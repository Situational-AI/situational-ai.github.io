The Definition

What Is a Situation?

A situation is the meaning of events and state, interpreted from the perspective of an observer, relative to goals, responsibilities, relationships, time, uncertainty, and possible action.

Event

What happened

A payment failed. A message arrived. A build broke.

State

How things are

The invoice is overdue. The customer is inactive. The branch is blocked.

Context

What is available to know

History, documents, roles, relationships, constraints, and current signals.

Intent

What an actor wants

Recover payment. Preserve trust. Ship safely. Learn the answer.

Situation

What it means now

The observer-relative interpretation that establishes what matters and what response is appropriate.

The same event and state can produce different situations for different people. Situation is not another piece of context. It is the organized meaning formed from context.

The Distinction

Context Is Necessary. It Is Not Enough.

Context engineering asks what information the model should receive. Situational AI asks what that information means, for whom, and what it calls for.

01

Situation as a Primitive

Modern AI often infers situations implicitly inside a prompt. We propose making situation explicit: representable, comparable, memorable, testable, and revisable.

02

Continuous Recognition

Sense signals from conversations, events, timers, and changing data; detect which situations are emerging, evolving, overlapping, or ending.

03

Observer-Relative Meaning

The same facts can mean different things to a customer, salesperson, operator, or system because their goals and responsibilities differ.

04

Act, Ask, Wait, or Observe

Recognition establishes what the moment calls for—including the judgment that no immediate action is appropriate.

05

Situation Memory

Remember not merely facts and conversations, but what was recognized, what response was chosen, why it was chosen, and what outcome followed.

06

Situational Embeddings

Represent underlying dynamics so structurally analogous situations can be found across different vocabularies, workflows, and domains.
