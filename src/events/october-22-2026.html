The World Does Not Arrive
as Prompts.

AI has become remarkably capable at responding to prompts, but the world does not arrive as prompts. Situational AI is an emerging research program exploring whether “situation” can become a first-class computational primitive—helping systems recognize what is happening, why it matters, what response is called for, and learn from what happens next.

Summer 2026

From a Hypothesis to Five Student Explorations

During the summer of 2026, five UT Dallas students worked with Mindspace AI to explore different questions emerging from Situational AI.

They examined its theoretical foundations, compared it with existing models of situation awareness, investigated whether explicit situation recognition could reduce computational context, and built working applications incorporating situation-centric concepts.

On October 22, we are bringing those explorations together—and opening the questions to a broader community.
