Bring Us a Real Situation

Some of the most important research questions emerge from real operational environments. Organizations can collaborate with Mindspace AI and Situational AI researchers around problems involving changing conditions, situation recognition, memory, coordination and intelligent response.

Customer disengagement

A relationship going quiet before anyone has called it a problem.

Document and workflow breakdown

A process that is still “running” while the situation it serves has changed.

Emerging operational risk

Signals that only mean something together, and only for a particular observer.

Changing customer intent

The same words carrying a different situation than they did last week.

Escalation recognition

Knowing when to act, ask, wait, or hand off to a person.

Multi-party coordination

One event, several observers, several situations at once.
