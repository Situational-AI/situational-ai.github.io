Situational AI &
Situation Awareness

Evolution of the idea, related models and Endsley's Situation Awareness framework.

Krishang Reddy Mandala · UT Dallas · Summer 2026 · Presented at The World Does Not Arrive as Prompts, October 22, 2026

One of five explorations from the summer of 2026, when UT Dallas students worked with Mindspace AI on questions emerging from Situational AI. This one traces the evolution of the idea, the models related to it, and how it relates to Endsley's Situation Awareness framework.

This is the permanent page for the work. It will be filled in with the student's own account, materials, and outcomes as they are supplied.

The Question

The research question in Krishang's own words: what this exploration set out to establish about Situational AI's relationship to existing models of situation awareness.

Approach

Sources examined, models compared, and how the comparison with Endsley's framework was structured.

Findings and Open Questions

What the comparison found, where Situational AI departs from prior models, and what remains unresolved.

Materials

Paper, slides, demo, repository, or recording, as they become available.
