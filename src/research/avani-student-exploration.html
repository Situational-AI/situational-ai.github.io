Exploration

The journey, questions and lessons emerging from the summer work.

Avani Tripathi · UT Dallas · Summer 2026 · Presented at The World Does Not Arrive as Prompts, October 22, 2026

One of five explorations from the summer of 2026, when UT Dallas students worked with Mindspace AI on questions emerging from Situational AI. This one steps back from any single project to describe the journey itself: the questions the students brought, the ones the work raised, and the lessons that emerged.

This is the permanent page for the work. It will be filled in with the student's own account, materials, and outcomes as they are supplied.
